# Очередь генерации гравюр

> Процедура и стиль-блоки: `docs/engravings.md`. Сгенерированное вычёркивать.

## 1. The Thing — гравюра-маска (жанр А)

Цель: `public/plates/monster-the-thing.png` → живая пластина бестиария +
маргиналия у острова Dagon'а. Референсы к генерации:
`public/plates/monster-cthulhu.png`, `public/plates/monster-tornasuk.png`.

```
Highly detailed black-and-white ink engraving in the manner of a 19th-century
natural-history plate: dense crosshatching and stippling, pure black India ink
on a plain pure-white background. A single isolated figure, complete and
uncropped, centered with clear white margins on every side. No frame, no
border, no caption, no lettering, no signature, no background scenery.
Square canvas.

Subject, drawn strictly after the text of H. P. Lovecraft's "Dagon" and
nothing else:

"With only a slight churning to mark its rise to the surface, the thing slid
into view above the dark waters. Vast, Polyphemus-like, and loathsome, it
darted like a stupendous monster of nightmares to the monolith, about which
it flung its gigantic scaly arms, the while it bowed its hideous head and
gave vent to certain measured sounds."

A colossal, loathsome sea-creature of vaguely man-like outline — kin to the
fish-like men of the monolith's carvings, "depicted disporting like fishes" —
rising hunched from a low ring of churning dark water at its base. Gigantic
scaly arms spread wide as if to embrace a monolith that is not shown; hideous
head bowed in worship, mouth open in measured chant. Scales rendered in dense
engraved hatching; webbed, clawed hands.
```

## 2. Plate VI «тварь у монолита» — вклейка (жанр Б)

Цель: `src/widgets/plates/monolith-rite.png` → страница
`locations/cyclopean-monolith` (миниатюра уйдёт в превью пина на карте и в
галерею рассказа). Референсы: `src/widgets/plates/rlyeh.png`,
`src/widgets/plates/swamp-ritual.png`.

```
A full-page wood-engraved book plate in the manner of Gustave Doré: fine
parallel hatching, deep rich blacks, dramatic light, engraved on lightly
toned antique paper, enclosed in a thin single-rule border like an
illustration from a 19th-century edition. No text, no caption, no lettering,
no signature anywhere on the plate.

Subject, drawn strictly after the text of H. P. Lovecraft's "Dagon" and
nothing else:

"With only a slight churning to mark its rise to the surface, the thing slid
into view above the dark waters. Vast, Polyphemus-like, and loathsome, it
darted like a stupendous monster of nightmares to the monolith, about which
it flung its gigantic scaly arms, the while it bowed its hideous head and
gave vent to certain measured sounds."

A moonlit chasm of the risen sea-bottom, slopes of black putrid ooze under
"the newly bestowed rays of the ascending moon". Across a channel of dark
water, a gigantic well-shaped monolith "that gleamed whitely", its surface
carved with aquatic hieroglyphics and enormous bas-reliefs of fish-like men.
A vast scaly sea-thing has flung its gigantic arms about the stone, hideous
head bowed against it in worship. On the near slope, a lone tiny human
figure watches, dwarfed to insignificance — the scene seen from behind him,
across the water.
```
